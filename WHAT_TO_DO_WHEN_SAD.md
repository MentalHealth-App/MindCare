# What to Do When App Detects "Sad" 😢

## 💙 Immediate Actions:

### 1. **Acknowledge Your Feelings**
   - It's okay to feel sad - this is a normal human emotion
   - Remember: feelings are temporary and don't define you
   - Give yourself permission to feel what you're feeling

### 2. **Use the App's Support Features**
   - Click **"💙 Get Support & Activities"** button that appears when Sad is detected
   - Access personalized:
     - 🧘 Meditation videos
     - 🎵 Calming music
     - 💫 Daily affirmations
     - 💡 Helpful tips
     - 📖 Inspirational quotes

### 3. **Talk to Someone**
   - Reach out to a trusted friend or family member
   - Talk to a mental health professional
   - Contact a crisis helpline if needed (see below)

### 4. **Practice Self-Care**
   - Take deep breaths (4-7-8 breathing technique)
   - Go for a gentle walk outside
   - Write in a journal about your feelings
   - Do something kind for yourself
   - Get enough rest and sleep

---

## 🆘 Crisis Resources (If Needed):

**National Suicide Prevention Lifeline:** `988`  
**Crisis Text Line:** Text `HOME` to `741741`  
**International Association for Suicide Prevention:** iasp.info

## ⚠️ When to Seek Professional Help:

Seek immediate professional help if you experience:
- Thoughts of self-harm or suicide
- Persistent sadness for 2+ weeks
- Difficulty functioning in daily life
- Loss of interest in activities you once enjoyed
- Changes in sleep or appetite
- Feelings of hopelessness or worthlessness

---

## 📊 Understanding Your Results:

**What "Sad" Detection Means:**
- Your voice shows lower pitch and volume
- This is a physiological indicator, not a diagnosis
- It's normal to experience sadness sometimes
- The app is designed to help you notice and address these feelings

**Remember:**
- Voice analysis is a tool, not a replacement for professional care
- One "Sad" detection doesn't mean you're depressed
- Patterns over time are more meaningful than single results
- Trust your own feelings and experiences
